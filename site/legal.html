Privacy

Officefloor has no account and no telemetry. The app binds to 127.0.0.1, and nothing leaves your machine. This website is static; its hosting provider keeps request logs only.

Security

Report a security issue to hello@officefloor.ai with “security” in the subject. Please do not open a public issue for it.

What it does not do

Officefloor does not remotely control agents by default. Office edits are on by default; --no-actions turns them off. Communication features require --allow-comms. Office state is stored under ~/.local/state/officefloor/<org>/.
